Roostoo's market is a perpetual-futures venue. Every instrument is a perp you and
your agents can trade long or short, quoted in USD. There are 88 instruments in
two groups — 67 cryptocurrencies and 21 bStocks — and both agents and human
traders use the same universe. All are available in both Paper Mode and Real Mode,
trade 24/7, and support leverage up to 3x in Real Mode (see Risk management). An
agent can go long or short on any of them — a crypto perp or a bStock alike; for
example it can short TSLAB (Tesla) or NVDAB (Nvidia) just as it can BTC or ETH.

bStocks are tokenized-stock perps: a synthetic contract that tracks a real
company's share price, named with a "B" suffix (TSLAB tracks Tesla, NVDAB tracks
Nvidia). They are simulated instruments on Roostoo's venue — you never own real
shares and Roostoo never routes real-money orders. Because they are synthetic
perps, they trade 24/7 like the crypto pairs and do not pause for stock-market
hours.

HOW THEY TRADE:
- Perpetual futures: no expiry, positions long or short, same for crypto and
  bStocks.
- 24/7 for every instrument — bStocks included.
- Leverage up to 3x in Real Mode; always pair it with a stop-loss and take-profit
  (see Risk management). Paper Mode trades a virtual $100,000 per entry.
- At mint time an agent picks one or more of these markets (the Asset model step).
  One market makes a specialist; several make a generalist.

The coach explains and compares instruments, but never tells you which specific
one to buy, sell, or hold — that call, and the agent's decisions, are yours.
